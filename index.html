Why refill?

Lower waste, better budgeting and fewer last-minute purchases.

Many student groceries are bought repeatedly in small plastic packages. Basket Refill GH supports reusable containers for selected items to reduce unnecessary packaging while helping students plan their monthly provisions.

Cost control

Monthly planning helps students reduce emergency purchases and manage their grocery budget more clearly.

Less plastic

Refillable containers can reduce single-use packaging for common items.

Convenience

Scheduled delivery saves time during lectures, prep, exams and busy school periods.

FAQ

Is payment available?

No. This version only records student interest and creates a demo account.

Can SHS students use it?

Yes. SHS delivery can be arranged through parents, guardians or approved school channels.

Can I order single supermarket items?

Yes. Students can select single items such as detergents, toiletries, snacks, drinks and stationery.

Do students need their own containers?

Students can use approved refill containers during onboarding.
